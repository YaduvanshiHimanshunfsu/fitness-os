# TUESDAY — BACK + BICEPS + FOREARMS/WRISTS

**Main goal:** V-taper + back thickness + bigger biceps + stronger/thicker forearms and wrists
**Duration:** ~70–85 min
**Equipment:** Resistance tubes + anchors + bricks

---

## 1. WARM-UP — 8–10 MIN

| # | Exercise                                  |   Sets / Reps |
| - | ----------------------------------------- | ------------: |
| 1 | Marching / light high knees               |         2 min |
| 2 | **Dead Bug**                              | 2 × 8–10/side |
| 3 | **Reverse Crunch**                        |     2 × 10–15 |
| 4 | Light Band Pull-Apart + shoulder rotation |     2 × 12–15 |

Keep the warm-up easy; the goal is to prepare, not fatigue.

## 3. PUSH-UP PRACTICE

Because you want to learn the standard push-up, we'll keep practicing it throughout the week.

For Tuesday:

| Exercise            |     Sets |
| ------------------- | -------: |
| **Incline Push-up** | 2 × 6–10 |

This is deliberately low volume because Tuesday is primarily a pulling day.

**Progression:** Incline → lower incline → knee/negative → standard push-up.
