# SATURDAY — BACK + CHEST + ARMS + FOREARMS

**Main goal:** V-taper + additional chest volume + arm development + forearm/wrist strength
**Duration:** ~70–85 min
**Intensity:** Moderate. Wednesday remains your hardest day.

---

## 1. WARM-UP — 8–10 MIN

| # | Exercise                            |   Sets / Reps | Purpose                   |
| - | ----------------------------------- | ------------: | ------------------------- |
| 1 | Marching / light high knees         |         2 min | Raise temperature         |
| 2 | **Dead Bug**                        | 2 × 8–10/side | Core activation           |
| 3 | **Heel Taps**                       |     2 × 16–20 | Core activation           |
| 4 | Light Band Pull-Apart + arm circles |     2 × 12–15 | Shoulder/back preparation |

---

## 2. MAIN WORKOUT

### BACK — MAIN PRIORITY

| #     | Exercise                          |   Sets × Reps |   Rest | Easier Alternative    |
| ----- | --------------------------------- | ------------: | -----: | --------------------- |
| **1** | **Resistance-Tube Lat Pulldown**  | **3 × 10–15** | 90 sec | Straight-arm pulldown |
| **2** | **Resistance-Tube Bent-Over Row** | **3 × 10–15** | 90 sec | Brick bent-over row   |
| **3** | **Face Pull**                     | **3 × 12–20** | 60 sec | Band pull-apart       |

These give you another weekly stimulus for your **lats, upper back and rear delts**, which is important for the V-taper.

### CHEST

| #     | Exercise                        |   Sets × Reps |      Rest | Easier Alternative |
| ----- | ------------------------------- | ------------: | --------: | ------------------ |
| **4** | **Push-up progression**         |  **3 × 5–10** | 75–90 sec | Incline push-up    |
| **5** | **Chest Fly — Resistance Tube** | **2 × 12–15** | 60–75 sec | Light-band fly     |

> Do push-ups **before the fly** so you can practice the movement while fresh.

**Push-up progression:** Incline → lower incline → negatives → standard push-up → decline push-up. Don't rush.
