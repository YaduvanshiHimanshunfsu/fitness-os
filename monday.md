# MONDAY — CHEST + TRICEPS

**Target session:** ~65–85 minutes
**Order:** Warm-up → Main workout → Post-workout
**Primary equipment:** Resistance tubes + door anchor + push-up handles
**Secondary:** 3–4 kg bricks

---

## 1. WARM-UP — 8–10 MINUTES

| Exercise                         | Sets / Time | Purpose                                  |
| -------------------------------- | ----------: | ---------------------------------------- |
| Marching / light high knees      |       2 min | Raise body temperature                   |
| Arm circles + shoulder rotations |       1 min | Shoulder mobility                        |
| Band Pull-Apart                  |   2 × 12–15 | Activate upper back/rear delts           |
| Scapular Push-up                 |    2 × 8–10 | Prepare shoulders/scapula                |
| **Incline Push-up**              |    1 × 8–10 | Prepare chest + practice push-up pattern |

**Do not fatigue yourself here.** Warm-up should make you feel better, not weaker.

---

## 2. MAIN WORKOUT — 7 EXERCISES

### Priority: Chest → Push-up skill → Triceps

| #     | Exercise                              |   Sets × Reps |      Rest | Difficulty / Alternative                                             |
| ----- | ------------------------------------- | ------------: | --------: | -------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **1** | **Chest Press — Resistance Tube**     | **3 × 10–15** |    90 sec | Main chest strength movement. **Alternative:** brick/floor press     |
| **2** | **Push-up Progression**               |  **3 × 5–10** |    90 sec | Main skill movement. Use the level you can perform with clean form   |
| **3** | **Incline Chest Press — Tube**        | **3 × 10–15** | 75–90 sec | Upper-chest emphasis. **Alternative:** brick incline press           |
| **4** | **Chest Fly — Tube**                  | **3 × 12–15** | 60–75 sec | Controlled stretch + squeeze                                         |
| **5** | **Incline Chest Fly — Tube**          | **2 × 12–15** | 60–75 sec | Upper-chest isolation. **Alternative:** lighter normal fly           |
| **6** | **Triceps Pushdown — Tube**           | **3 × 10–15** | 60–75 sec | Main triceps movement. **Alternative:** close-grip push-up           |
| **7** | **Overhead Triceps Extension — Tube** | **3 × 10–15** | 60–75 sec | Long-head triceps. **Alternative:** one/two-brick overhead extension |

### Volume Summary

| Muscle Group    | Working Sets |
| --------------- | -----------: |
| **Chest**       |           11 |
| **Triceps**     |            6 |
| **Push-up practice** |      included |

That's enough. Do **not** add another 2–3 chest exercises on Monday.

---

## 3. POST-WORKOUT — 10–12 MINUTES

| Exercise                 |    Sets / Time | Purpose                    |
| ------------------------ | -------------: | -------------------------- |
| Doorway Chest Stretch    |     2 × 30 sec | Chest recovery             |
| Overhead Triceps Stretch | 2 × 30 sec/arm | Triceps mobility           |
| Lat Stretch              |     2 × 30 sec | Shoulder/lat mobility      |
| **Wall Slides**          |         2 × 10 | Posture / shoulder control |
| **Lateral Band Walk**    |    2 × 10/side | Hip/knee control           |
