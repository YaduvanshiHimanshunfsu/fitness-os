# WEDNESDAY — LEGS + ATHLETIC STRENGTH + GRIP

**This is your toughest training day** because Thursday is your rest day.

**Main goals:** strong legs, glutes, athletic movement, knee control, core stability, powerful grip/forearms.

**Duration:** ~75–90 min

---

## 1. WARM-UP — 10–12 MIN

| # | Exercise                              |   Sets / Reps | Purpose                     |
| - | ------------------------------------- | ------------: | --------------------------- |
| 1 | Brisk marching / light high knees     |         2 min | Raise temperature           |
| 2 | **Dead Bug**                          | 2 × 8–10/side | Core activation             |
| 3 | **Plank**                             | 2 × 30–40 sec | Core bracing                |
| 4 | Bodyweight Squat + hip/ankle mobility |        2 × 10 | Prepare hips, knees, ankles |

**Important:** No hard burpees or intense conditioning before the leg workout.

---

## 2. MAIN WORKOUT — LEGS

| #     | Exercise                              |      Sets × Reps |       Rest | Easier Alternative      |
| ----- | ------------------------------------- | ---------------: | ---------: | ----------------------- |
| **1** | **Resistance-Tube Squat**             |     **4 × 8–15** | 90–120 sec | Bodyweight squat        |
| **2** | **Bulgarian Split Squat**             | **3 × 8–12/leg** |     90 sec | Reverse lunge           |
| **3** | **Resistance-Tube Romanian Deadlift** |    **4 × 10–15** |     90 sec | Brick RDL               |
| **4** | **Reverse Lunge**                     | **3 × 8–12/leg** |  75–90 sec | Static split squat      |
| **5** | **Glute Bridge**                      |    **3 × 12–20** |  60–75 sec | Bodyweight glute bridge |
| **6** | **Side Lunge**                        |  **2 × 10/side** |  60–75 sec | Assisted side lunge     |
| **7** | **Calf Raise**                        |    **4 × 15–25** |  45–60 sec | Bodyweight calf raise   |

### Why this combination?

- **Squat:** overall leg strength
- **Bulgarian split squat:** unilateral strength + stability
- **RDL:** hamstrings/glutes + posterior chain
- **Reverse lunge:** legs + balance
- **Glute bridge:** glute strength
- **Side lunge:** frontal-plane strength/mobility
- **Calf raise:** lower-leg strength

---

## 3. ATHLETIC FINISHER

Choose **ONE**, not both.

| Exercise             |          Sets |
| -------------------- | ------------: |
| **Burpees**          |      3 × 6–10 |
| **OR Fast Step-Ups** | 3 × 30–40 sec |

Start with **fast step-ups** if you become excessively fatigued after the leg workout. Progress to burpees as conditioning improves.

---

## 4. GRIP + FOREARMS

| Exercise                    |              Sets |
| --------------------------- | ----------------: |
| **Farmer Hold with Bricks** | **3 × 30–60 sec** |
| **Wrist Curl**              |     **2 × 15–20** |
| **Reverse Wrist Curl**      |     **2 × 15–20** |

Keep these controlled. Wednesday is already a heavy session.

---
