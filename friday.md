# FRIDAY — SHOULDERS + CHEST + TRICEPS

**Main goal:** broader shoulders + upper chest + triceps
**Duration:** ~70–85 min
**Equipment:** Resistance tubes primarily, push-up handles, bricks
**Intensity:** Moderate–hard; Wednesday remains your toughest day.

---

## 1. WARM-UP — 8–10 MIN

| # | Exercise                            |   Sets / Reps | Purpose                |
| - | ----------------------------------- | ------------: | ---------------------- |
| 1 | Marching / light high knees         |         2 min | Raise body temperature |
| 2 | **Dead Bug**                        | 2 × 8–10/side | Core activation        |
| 3 | **Reverse Crunch**                  |     2 × 10–15 | Core/ab activation     |
| 4 | Light Band Pull-Apart + arm circles |     2 × 12–15 | Shoulder preparation   |

Keep everything easy; save your energy for the main work.

---
