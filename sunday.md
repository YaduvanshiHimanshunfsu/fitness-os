# SUNDAY — FULL BODY ATHLETIC + CONDITIONING

**Main goal:** athletic physique, full-body strength, conditioning, movement quality, core stability
**Intensity:** Moderate — Wednesday remains your hardest day
**Duration:** ~65–80 min

---

## 1. WARM-UP — 8–10 MIN

| # | Exercise                                |   Sets / Reps | Purpose                |
| - | --------------------------------------- | ------------: | ---------------------- |
| 1 | Marching / light high knees             |         2 min | Raise body temperature |
| 2 | **Bird Dog**                            | 2 × 8–10/side | Core + trunk stability |
| 3 | **Plank**                               | 2 × 30–45 sec | Core bracing           |
| 4 | Bodyweight Reverse Lunge + hip mobility |     2 × 8/leg | Prepare hips/knees     |

Keep this easy and controlled.

---

## 2. MAIN WORKOUT — FULL BODY

| #     | Exercise                                   |       Sets × Reps |      Rest | Easier Alternative        |
| ----- | ------------------------------------------ | ----------------: | --------: | ------------------------- |
| **1** | **Resistance-Tube Lat Pulldown**           |     **3 × 10–15** |    90 sec | Straight-arm pulldown     |
| **2** | **Resistance-Tube Chest Press**            |     **3 × 10–15** |    90 sec | Incline push-up           |
| **3** | **Resistance-Tube Romanian Deadlift**      |     **3 × 10–15** |    90 sec | Brick RDL                 |
| **4** | **Step-Ups**                               |    **3 × 10/leg** | 60–90 sec | Low-step step-ups         |
| **5** | **Resistance-Tube Shoulder/Lateral Raise** |     **3 × 12–20** |    60 sec | Light brick lateral raise |
| **6** | **Push-up progression**                    |      **3 × 5–10** |    75 sec | Incline push-up           |
| **7** | **Farmer Hold**                            | **3 × 30–60 sec** | 60–90 sec | Lighter bricks            |

### Why these?

- **Pulldown:** V-taper
- **Chest press:** chest
- **RDL:** posterior-chain strength
- **Step-ups:** unilateral leg strength + athletic movement
- **Lateral raises:** shoulder width
- **Push-ups:** skill + upper-body endurance
- **Farmer hold:** grip, wrists, forearms, whole-body stability

This is intentionally **not another huge bodybuilding workout**. Sunday should make you feel athletic, not destroyed.

---

## 3. ATHLETIC CONDITIONING

Choose **ONE**:

| Option                | Work          |
| --------------------- | ------------- |
| **Burpees**           | 3 × 6–10      |
| **Fast Step-Ups**     | 3 × 30–40 sec |
| **Mountain Climbers** | 3 × 30 sec    |

Start with **fast step-ups**. Once your conditioning improves, use burpees. Don't do all three.
